Price office and commercial cleaning contracts in minutes — by floor area in m² (or sq ft), cleaning frequency and production rates — and send a professional two-page proposal with the monthly fee, scope of work and terms.

Made for cleaning companies bidding on offices, shops, clinics, gyms and small commercial sites in the UK, Ireland, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, the US and Canada.

★ HOW THE PRICE IS BUILT
Area × frequency ÷ production rate (m² per cleaner-hour) → hours per week → hours per month, plus periodic services and supervision time → labour, supplies and overheads → your target margin → a monthly fee (with minimum fee and rounding). Consumables you supply are added with a handling mark-up.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE — 6 connected sheets
• Bid Calculator — up to 12 areas and 8 periodic services; monthly fee, VAT, annual contract value, hours per week, busiest visit, team size for your cleaning window, price per m², profit per month
• Client Proposal — two A4 pages filled in automatically: areas and frequencies, monthly fee, scope of work for each area type, contract terms and signature lines
• Settings — 12 area types with production rates and task lists, 7 frequencies, traffic levels, 8 periodic services, wages, on-costs, supervision, supplies, overheads, margin, VAT/GST, minimum fee, contract clauses
• Break-even — your monthly fixed costs and hours → the minimum rate per labour hour
• Bid Log — win rate, won monthly revenue, open pipeline, overdue follow-ups
• Start Here — setup steps and bidding tips
