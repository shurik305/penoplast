Price every cleaning job in about a minute — by floor area in m² (or sq ft), with VAT/GST, extras, travel and your profit worked out for you. Then send a professional A4 quote to your client.

Made for small cleaning companies and self-employed cleaners in the UK, Ireland, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, the US and Canada. Most pricing templates only work in square feet and dollars — this one is metric-first, with VAT/GST built in.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE — 7 connected sheets
• Quote Calculator — floor area or bedrooms → labour hours, time on site, price, VAT, price per m² and your profit on the job
• Client Quote — a print-ready A4 quote, filled in automatically (save as PDF and send)
• Price List Builder — standard prices by property size for 7 services, plus weekly, fortnightly and monthly rates
• Break-even — your monthly fixed costs and hours → the minimum hourly rate you must charge
• Settings — wages, on-costs, supplies, overheads, margin, VAT/GST, minimum charge, travel, rounding
• Quote Log — track every quote: win rate, open pipeline, overdue follow-ups
• Start Here — 5-minute setup guide and pricing tips

Regular clean, deep clean, end of tenancy / move-out, after builders, office regular clean, office deep clean, short-let / holiday-let turnover. Each has a "what's included" checklist that prints on the quote.
