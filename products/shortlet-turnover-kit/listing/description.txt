Everything a short-let turnover needs in one kit: a printable 40+ point cleaning checklist, a turnover log that works out minutes and cleaner pay, a cleaning fee calculator, a restock shopping list and a damage & issue report.

For hosts and co-hosts of holiday lets, serviced apartments and short-term rentals on any platform (for example Airbnb or Vrbo) — and for the cleaners who turn them over.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE
• Turnover Checklist — 7 sections (arrival, kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors & touch points, final checks), 40+ tasks, restock and issue notes, sign-off. Print from the workbook per property, or use the blank PDF.
• Turnover Log — date, property, cleaner, start and finish time → minutes and pay calculated (fixed per turnover or hourly), issues flagged, unpaid pay totalled, turnovers this month, average minutes.
• Cleaning Fee Calculator — what one turnover really costs you: cleaner, laundry (beds × cost per linen set), guest consumables (per stay, guest, bathroom or bedroom) and supplies — plus the cleaning fee that covers it, with your mark-up and any platform or payment fee. All properties compared in one table.
• Restock List — count what's in the cupboard, choose how many stays to stock for, get the shopping list and total.
• Issue Report — record damage or missing items the same day, with photos and costs (printable).
• Settings — up to 10 properties, linen sets owned vs recommended, 15 common consumables plus your own.
